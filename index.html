STREAMLINED WORKFLOW

How Sahayak Works

A seamless 4-step framework transforming how community drives and disaster operations are coordinated.

01

Create Requirement

NGOs define volunteer roles, required certifications (e.g. First Aid, Triage), slot timings, and exact venue coordinates.

02

Match Volunteers

Sahayak's AI engine ranks candidate volunteers based on skill compatibility, travel distance, and availability in real time.

03

Dispatch & Schedule

Volunteers accept with 1 click, receive safety briefs, navigation routes, and automated task reminders.

04

Track Impact

Live geo-fenced clock-ins verify on-site attendance, completing tasks release authenticated service certificates.
